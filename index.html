Recovery

Optional, and deliberately outside the session above — run it on its own, after the mobility, while you are still warm. Cold damps the inflammatory signal that drives adaptation, so it is a trade: take it when the next run matters more than this one's gains — race week, back-to-back hard days, after a test. Skip the cold and take a plain warm bath after the sessions you want to adapt to: threshold, VO₂max, a long run with tempo in it.

Devices

Both are read over Bluetooth, straight from the device — nothing goes to Stryd and nothing leaves the phone. The strap is paired by the standard heart rate service, so any chest strap works, not only one brand. Chrome on Android can do this; Safari on iOS cannot, and says so rather than offering a button that does nothing. Pairing has to start from a tap: a page is not allowed to go looking for devices on its own.
